# AgoraNet Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AgoraNet exists to support serious, humane civic participation. We welcome
contributors, users, researchers, and critics who help make the project more
truthful, safe, and useful. Participation must be respectful of the dignity,
privacy, and safety of others.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas rigorously without attacking people.
- Make room for good-faith disagreement and changing one's mind.
- Protect private information and report security concerns through the proper
  private channel.
- Give credit for others' work and communicate limitations honestly.
- Help newcomers participate without gatekeeping or humiliation.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Doxxing, sharing credentials or personal data, or attempting to link an
  AgoraNet Alias to a True Self.
- Deliberate deception about security findings, test results, provenance, or
  project governance.
- Spam, manipulation, or attempts to capture project decisions through
  coercion, deception, or undisclosed conflicts of interest.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security advisory
channel or to the current maintainers. Reports will be handled with care for
privacy and fairness. Maintainers may warn, moderate discussion, remove
content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action.

This is an initial project policy. It will be reviewed openly as the maintainer
and contributor community grows.
