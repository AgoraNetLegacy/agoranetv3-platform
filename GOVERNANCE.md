# AgoraNet Project Governance

## Status: founding-maintainer period

AgoraNet's intended product governance belongs to its community. The open
source project is not yet large enough to operate through that future model.
Until a contributor community is established, the repository owner serves as
the **Founding Maintainer**: a steward responsible for keeping the project
safe, coherent, and moving, not a permanent owner of the platform's civic
authority.

This document governs the software project. It does not override the AgoraNet
Constitution, nor does it claim that the application has already handed all
platform authority to its users.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone may propose issues, documentation, designs, code, tests, and reviews.
Contributors have equal standing in discussion; acceptance of work depends on
scope, quality, safety, and fit with the project's published direction.

### Maintainers

Maintainers review and merge changes, manage releases, and respond to security
reports. They must make decisions in the open whenever doing so does not expose
a security issue, personal data, or another legitimate confidential concern.

### Founding Maintainer

During this initial period, the repository owner appoints maintainers, resolves
deadlocks, and is accountable for release decisions. This authority is limited
by the project documents, transparent decision records, contributor review, and
the commitment to establish a broader maintainer model as the project matures.

## How decisions are made

1. Material proposals begin in an issue or discussion.
2. Contributors identify affected users, privacy implications, security
   consequences, and relevant constitutional or product rules.
3. A pull request implements an agreed direction and is reviewed by a
   maintainer before merge.
4. Significant decisions are recorded in repository documentation or release
   notes so they can be revisited.

Urgent security and incident decisions may be made privately first, then
documented once users are protected.

## Protected areas

Changes affecting identity, privacy, moderation, permanence, the ledger,
wallets, economics, production operations, or the Constitution require explicit
maintainer review and may require additional design discussion or testing.

## Path to shared stewardship

The founding-maintainer period is temporary. The project will publish a revised
governance process once it has multiple active maintainers and a demonstrated
contributor base. That revision should define maintainer selection, term and
removal processes, conflict-of-interest rules, decision thresholds, and an
appeal path. It should be discussed publicly before adoption.
