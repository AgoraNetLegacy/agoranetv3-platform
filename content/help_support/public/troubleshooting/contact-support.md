Open Help and Support. You can ask the AI helpdesk in your own words, or select “Skip self-service and open a support request” to contact a human operator.

For a support request, state what you were trying to do, which page you were on, what happened, and any error message that does not contain a secret. Check screenshots before attaching or sharing them. Remove access keys, Humanity Credentials, wallet seed phrases, private messages, passwords, and unrelated personal information.

After submission, AgoraNet shows a case reference beginning with AN-. Save that reference. Signed-out users must provide a reply email; signed-in requests stay with the currently selected profile. Support can investigate and explain, but separate authorized processes decide verification, moderation, security, treasury, and other consequential changes.
