Your feed shows updates only from sources you chose, such as AgoraNet topics, Discussions, Circles, Chambers, and polls. Open Choose what feeds it, select or clear the checkboxes you want, and save your sources.

AgoraNet does not add items because you viewed, paused on, or clicked similar content. If you did not select a source, that source cannot enter your personal feed.

Feed choices belong only to the profile currently in use. Your True Self and Alias keep separate lists. People who are not signed in see a general public stream ordered by the same published formula for everyone.
