# AgoraNet Name and Mark Policy

## Purpose

The AgoraNet name, logo, and visual identity help people identify the official
project and distinguish it from independent software. This policy is about
names and branding. It does not restrict the rights granted by the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE) for the source code.

## Permitted references

You may use the AgoraNet name to make accurate, non-confusing statements about
this project, including to say that your work is based on, compatible with, or
contributed to AgoraNet. You may also use an unmodified AgoraNet logo when
linking to the official project or accurately identifying an unmodified build.

## Forks and modified versions

If you distribute, host, or promote a modified version, you must use a name and
branding that clearly distinguish it from AgoraNet. Do not call the modified
version "AgoraNet," use an AgoraNet logo as its primary identity, or imply that
it is official, endorsed, operated, or governed by the AgoraNet project.

You may say, for example, "based on AgoraNet" or "a fork of AgoraNet," provided
the statement is accurate and your own name is at least as prominent.

## Prohibited uses

Do not use the project name or marks in a way that is likely to confuse people
about source, affiliation, endorsement, security review, governance authority,
or custody of funds or data. Do not use them for scams, impersonation, or
misleading token, wallet, investment, or hosting claims.

## Permission and future stewardship

This is a lightweight project policy, not a claim of registered trademark
status. It may be revised through the project's published governance process as
the contributor community develops. If you need permission for a use not
covered here, open a public discussion unless the request is security-sensitive.
